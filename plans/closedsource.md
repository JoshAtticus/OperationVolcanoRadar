### Paid Ads
Yep, Kyle announced paid ads for Blaze :|. The ads will cost $5-$25. They'll essentially work exactly the same way Google Ads work by targetting individual groups of people.

<img width="1308" alt="Screenshot 2024-04-03 at 19 15 05" src="https://github.com/JoshAtticus/ProjectVolcanoRadar/assets/72828296/74222207-8bb2-4910-840a-e4e221fe8ce2">

But but but! Here's why *I* think this will fail. We'll use BetterAds for scale, it's a little ads program by BetterMeower that allows people to make ads that show up on other people's websites. They don't make money, they're for promotion of services. Now that that's out of the way, let's look at the statistics.

In the last 30 days, BetterAds had 4 impressions in total, or 0.13 per day, yes, not much, womp womp, anyways in that same time period only 3 posts were made on Blaze, or 0.1 per day (excluding the lovely post I created under ilikebalze), even more embarrasing. Let's say 3 quarters of Blaze's diminishing users use the updated platform and 1.1 ads are viewed per month, a whole 13 ads and 20% of another would have been seen, in a year. Does that seem worth it for your $12? Meanwhile BetterAds will cost you nothing and in a year, 13 ads and 56% of another would have been viewed.

Because of this, these paid ads aren't likely to stick around for long if they're even ever released.
